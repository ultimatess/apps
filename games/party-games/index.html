How Pass-the-Phone Works

Party Deck games are designed for in-person groups around a table using just ONE single phone!

  • Add all player names before starting.
  • Pass the phone to the named player when prompted.
  • Tap "I am [Player]" to unlock your personal privacy gate.
  • Tap to reveal your secret role, word, or location privately (hide it anytime with 1 tap).
  • Tap 'Done & Pass' to hand over to the next person with zero accidental spoilers!
  • Anti-repeat system guarantees places and drawing words never repeat.
